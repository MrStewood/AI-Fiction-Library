# Story Bible (Slim)

## Characters
- **Maren** — potion maker. External goal: maintain professional distance. Internal need: accept grief as evidence of love, not damage. Wound: lost someone years ago, never processed it. Arc: from detachment to reckoning.
- **Elias** — customer. Wants to forget his dead wife. Catalyst, not antagonist. Does not change.

## World rules
- Emotion potions exist. One rule: to forget an emotion, surrender it entirely. No partial versions.
- Cost: memories remain but lose emotional weight. Irreversible.
- Ingredients: grounded, non-precious. Craft is manual, physical.
- Shop is a real place with real inventory. Maren can refuse any request.

## Locations
1. Maren's potion shop (primary). Narrow, warm light, shelves of bottles, counter, back room.
2. The street outside (secondary, minimal). Used for final image only.

## Timeline
One evening. A few hours. Elias arrives near dusk; conversation stretches into full dark.

## Key setup/payoff
| ID | Setup | Payoff |
|---|---|---|
| SP-001 | Maren's hands move with practiced ease | Hands hesitate when making the grief potion |
| SP-002 | A bottle on a high shelf, never touched | Implied to be her own unused potion |
| SP-003 | Shop smell (herbs, vinegar, metallic) | Triggers memory at the end |
| SP-004 | Professional questions about grief | Questions become personal |
| SP-005 | Elias's specific request | Makes the cost concrete |

## Ending
Maren makes the potion. Elias drinks it. Memories remain, flat and empty. Maren does not drink her own potion. Final image: Maren at the shop door, holding a bottle she will not open.

## Tone
Contemplative, intimate, melancholic but not bleak. PG-13. No sentimentality, no tidy moral.
