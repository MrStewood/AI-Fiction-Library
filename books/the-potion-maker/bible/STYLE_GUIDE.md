# Style Guide

- Narrative distance: Close third-person, locked to Maren. The reader sees what she sees, hears what she hears. No head-hopping. No omniscient commentary.
- Sentence-length tendencies: Varied. Long, flowing sentences for description and sensory detail. Short, sharp sentences for emotional beats and turning points. The contrast is deliberate.
- Vocabulary level: Grounded, concrete, precise. No ornamental language. No fantasy-lexicon invention. Words earn their place through specificity, not sound.
- Dialogue style: Subtext-heavy. Characters deflect, hesitate, change the subject, focus on physical tasks while talking. No exposition through dialogue. No fact-sharing ping-pong.
- Humor level: Near zero. The tone is contemplative and intimate. If humor appears, it is dry and understated — a professional observation, not a joke.
- Imagery density: High, but restrained. Sensory detail is concrete (smells, textures, light, sound) and earns its place through emotional resonance, not decoration.
- Description density: Moderate. The shop is described through how Maren interacts with it, not through static inventory. The reader should feel the space, not tour it.
- Interior-monologue density: Low to moderate. Maren's interiority is shown through action and behavior, not through sustained reflection. The reader infers what she feels from what she does.
- Exposition strategy: Embedded in action. The magic system is explained through how Maren works — what she asks, what she weighs, what she sets aside. No info-dumps. No narrative aside.
- Chapter-opening tendencies: N/A (short story, no chapters)
- Chapter-closing tendencies: N/A (short story, no chapters)
- Prohibited stylistic habits: Sentimentality. Direct emotional labeling ("She felt sad"). Fantasy-lexicon invention. Expository dialogue. Tidy moral conclusions. Dramatic irony that undermines character agency.
- POV / tense rules: Close third-person, past tense. Maren only. No exceptions.
- Repetition watch list: The word "grief" — use it sparingly. Let the emotion carry its own weight through action and image. The smell of the shop — repeat only for structural echoes (opening and closing), not for filler.
- Content / rating bounds: PG-13 default unless brief overrides. Emotional intensity permitted; physical violence, explicit sex, or gore are not.

## MICRO-CRAFT RULES
- The Physical Anchor: Characters must interact with their environment. Do not state emotions directly (e.g., 'She felt anxious'). Show interaction (e.g., 'She pressed her thumbnail into the edge of the laminated folder until the plastic snapped').
- Dialogue Economy: Characters deflect, change the subject, or focus on physical tasks while talking. Ban ping-pong fact-sharing dialogue.
- Sentence Architecture: Actively vary sentence lengths. Follow long, flowing descriptive sentences with a single, sharp sentence.
- Exemplars: This story's positive exemplar is the compressed intimacy of Raymond Carver, but in a fantasy register — the emotional precision of Kelly Link, the moral ambiguity of Sofia Samatar, the grounded magic of Ursula K. Le Guin. Banned patterns: sentimentality, tidy moral lessons, fantasy-lexicon invention, exposition through dialogue.
