# Scene Contract: SCENE-001

- **Scene ID:** SCENE-001
- **Chapter:** 1
- **POV character:** Maren
- **Present characters:** Maren, Departing Customer (minor, non-speaking), Elias (enters at end)
- **Character names:** Maren, Elias
- **Time:** Evening, near dusk
- **Location:** Maren's potion shop (primary) — narrow, shelves of bottles, counter, back room visible
- **Scene goal:** Establish Maren's professional world and emotional baseline; plant thematic question; introduce Elias's disruptive request
- **Opposition:** The potion itself exists and works — its irreversibility is the latent opposition that surfaces when Elias arrives
- **Opening condition:** Maren is alone, finishing a transaction, counting payment. The shop is her territory. She is in control.
- **Conflict progression:** (1) Maren's routine is calm, practiced, professional — she manages grief for others at a distance. (2) The departing customer's offhand remark about "setting things down" plants the thematic question. (3) Maren deflects — professional, controlled. (4) The bell above the door. Elias enters. He asks for the grief-dissolution potion. Not grief in general — grief for his wife. He wants to forget her. Not the facts — the feeling. (5) Maren's routine is broken. She begins her standard assessment protocol, but the request lands differently.
- **Turning point:** Elias specifies: not grief in general — grief for his wife. She died. He wants to forget her. The specificity cracks the professional surface.
- **Outcome:** Maren's equilibrium is disrupted. She begins professional assessment, but the request is personal in a way her routine cannot absorb.
- **Closing condition:** Maren is behind the counter, facing Elias, beginning her standard questions. The routine is broken. The story's engine has started.
- **Emotional change:** Professional calm → disrupted routine → the first crack of personal engagement
- **Information revealed:** The shop's mechanics (potions have names, prices, effects); Maren's competence and authority; the grief-dissolution potion exists and has a cost; Elias wants to forget his dead wife specifically
- **Information withheld:** Maren's own grief (unconscious/avoidant); the full cost of the potion (established in SCENE-002); the significance of the bottle on the high shelf
- **Setup / payoff IDs:** SP-001 (hands move with practiced ease), SP-002 (bottle on high shelf — background detail), SP-003 (shop smell grounded), SP-004 (professional questions established), SP-005 (Elias's specific request), SP-006 ("set things down" line), SP-007 (ingredients visible on counter — charcoal, vinegar, pale powder)
- **Character knowledge inputs:** Maren knows the potion system, its costs, its irreversibility. Elias knows what he wants and why.
- **Character knowledge outputs:** Reader learns the world's rules, Maren's role, and the specific request. Elias's story is partially revealed (wife died, grief is raw).
- **Continuity dependencies:** None (opening scene). Establishes the baseline for all subsequent scenes.
- **Forbidden revelations:** Maren's own grief must not be named or directly implied to Elias. The bottle on the shelf must not be explained. The full cost of the potion is reserved for SCENE-002.
- **Target word count:** 850
- **Causal link to next scene:** Maren has begun her professional assessment. SCENE-002 continues the conversation — her questions deepen, her distance cracks, and she commits to preparing the ingredients.
- **Functions served:** plot (establishes world, introduces catalyst), relationship (Maren-Elias dynamic begins), arc (baseline: professional detachment), setup-payoff (SP-001 through SP-007 planted)

- **PHYSICAL_SPACE:** Narrow shop. Shelves of bottles — glass, labeled, some dark, some clear. A counter between Maren and the customer. A mortar and pestle. The smell: herbs, vinegar, faint metallic. Warm but not inviting light. A bell above the door. Back room visible through a doorway.
- **BEATS_REQUIRED:** (1) Maren finishes transaction with departing customer. (2) Customer's "set things down" line. (3) Maren deflects. (4) Bell rings. Elias enters. (5) Elias makes his request — specific, flat, personal. (6) Maren begins professional assessment.
- **BANNED_PATTERNS:** No interiority dumps. No "she thought" attributions. No chant-like repetition of the shop smell. The departing customer must not be named — he is functional, not a character.
- **CRAFT_STARTERS:** Maren, Elias
