# Scene Contract: SCENE-004

- **Scene ID:** SCENE-004
- **Chapter:** 1
- **POV character:** Maren
- **Present characters:** Maren, Elias
- **Character names:** Maren, Elias
- **Time:** Evening, full dark — deep into the night
- **Location:** Maren's potion shop (counter, then back room for preparation)
- **Scene goal:** Maren makes the potion. The act of making it is the internal change made visible — her hands move deliberately, not with professional ease. The potion is finished and irreversible.
- **Opposition:** The potion itself — its irreversibility, and the fact that Maren has the same request buried inside her
- **Opening condition:** Maren has committed to making the potion. She tells Elias it will take time. She begins.
- **Conflict progression:** (1) Maren tells Elias she can make the potion. She says it will take time. (2) She begins: grinding, mixing, measuring. The ingredients are ordinary. The act is physical, deliberate. (3) Elias watches. Maren's hands do not hesitate — but the reader knows, from the midpoint, that she is making something she has considered making for herself. (4) The act of making it is also an act of confronting her own avoidance. (5) The potion is finished. It sits on the counter between them. (6) Maren looks at it. Her hands are still, heavy, resting on the counter. (7) She has done this. (8) There is a moment of silence. (9) Maren does not pick up the bottle. She slides it across the counter to Elias.
- **Turning point:** The potion is finished. It sits on the counter. Maren slides it to Elias. The choice is irreversible — not because of magic, but because she has made it.
- **Outcome:** The potion exists. It cannot be unmade. Maren has done the thing she was avoiding — she has participated in the destruction of something she knows has value.
- **Closing condition:** The potion is on the counter between them. Elias can take it or leave it. Maren has done her part. The cost is now shared.
- **Emotional change:** Deliberate action → heavy hands → the weight of what she's done → stillness → the slide across the counter
- **Information revealed:** Maren's hands move differently when making this potion — not with practiced ease but with deliberate weight. The potion is physical, real, irreversible. The ingredients are ordinary; the effect is not.
- **Information withheld:** Maren's internal processing — shown through physical stillness, not interiority. The reader sees her hands; the reader does not hear her thoughts.
- **Setup / payoff IDs:** SP-001 (hands move — but now heavy, not light), SP-003 (shop smell — now the smell of what she has done), SP-002 (bottle on shelf — she has made another one, but she will not make one for herself)
- **Character knowledge inputs:** Maren knows the potion system and its cost. She has chosen to make it despite her personal stake. Elias knows what he is asking for.
- **Character knowledge outputs:** Reader sees the act of making — the physical reality of the potion. The reader understands that Maren is doing something she has considered doing to herself.
- **Continuity dependencies:** Depends on SCENE-003 (cost explained, midpoint landed). The ingredients laid out in SCENE-003 are now being used. The bottle on the shelf (SP-002) is visible in background — she has made another, but not for herself.
- **Forbidden revelations:** Maren must not name her own grief. She must not explain why her hands move differently — only show it. The silence between them must not be filled with interiority.
- **Target word count:** 900
- **Causal link to next scene:** The potion is finished and on the counter. SCENE-005 continues: Elias drinks it. The grief leaves him. Maren faces the aftermath.
- **Functions served:** plot (bad guys close in / all is lost / dark night), relationship (Maren and Elias share the weight), arc (acting against avoidance), setup-payoff (SP-001 hands heavy, SP-003 smell changed, SP-002 parallel made)

- **PHYSICAL_SPACE:** Counter. Ingredients on the surface — charcoal dust, vinegar residue, pale powder. The potion: a small bottle, clear liquid, ordinary-looking. Maren's hands on the counter — still, heavy. Elias standing across from her. The shop is quiet. The smell is the same but it means something different now.
