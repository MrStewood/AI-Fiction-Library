# Scene Contract: SCENE-005

- **Scene ID:** SCENE-005
- **Chapter:** 1
- **POV character:** Maren
- **Present characters:** Maren, Elias
- **Character names:** Maren, Elias
- **Time:** Night — the conversation has lasted hours
- **Location:** Maren's potion shop (counter) → doorway (threshold between shop and street)
- **Scene goal:** Elias drinks the potion; the grief leaves him; Maren faces the aftermath and makes her final choice — she keeps her grief, stands at the threshold, carries what she will not set down
- **Opposition:** The irreversibility of what has happened — Maren cannot undo Elias's choice, and she cannot undo her own
- **Opening condition:** The potion is on the counter between them. Elias can take it or leave it. Maren has slid it across.
- **Conflict progression:** (1) Elias looks at the bottle. He does not touch it immediately. (2) He looks at Maren. He says something simple — perhaps "Thank you," perhaps nothing. (3) He takes the bottle. He drinks it. (4) The reader watches — Maren watches — as the grief leaves him. His face does not change. He sets the bottle down. (5) He looks at the shop — shelves, bottles, counter. He looks at Maren. (6) He says something ordinary — the weather, the time, the street outside. The grief is gone. The memories are flat. (7) He turns to leave. The bell rings. He is gone. (8) The shop is quiet. Maren stands behind the counter. (9) She looks at the bottle Elias drank from — empty, on the counter. (10) She looks at the bottle on the high shelf (SP-002) — the one she has never touched. (11) She reaches for it. Her hand closes around it. She holds it. (12) She does not open it. She sets it back. (13) She walks to the door. She stands in the doorway. She looks at the street. (14) She is holding the empty bottle from Elias's drink. She does not raise it. She does not drop it. She holds it.
- **Turning point:** Maren reaches for her own bottle on the high shelf and stops. She sets it back. This is the moment: she chooses to keep her grief rather than surrender it.
- **Outcome:** Elias has lost his grief. Maren has chosen to keep hers. The story's central question is embodied, not answered.
- **Closing condition:** Maren stands in the doorway of the shop. The street is dark. She is on the threshold — not inside, not outside. She holds the empty bottle. She does not drink. She does not cry. She stands there.
- **Emotional change:** Silence → Elias drinks → grief leaves him → Maren's ritual of letting go → she keeps hers → threshold image
- **Information revealed:** The potion works — grief is gone, memories are flat. Elias's transformation is visible through his ordinariness after drinking. Maren's final choice is shown through action: she reaches for the bottle and does not take it.
- **Information withheld:** Maren's internal processing — never named, only shown through physical action. The reader infers everything. The final image carries the weight without explanation.
- **Setup / payoff IDs:** SP-001 (hands — holding the empty bottle, not working), SP-002 (bottle on shelf — reached for, not taken), SP-003 (shop smell — behind her now, at the door), SP-004 (questions — replaced by silence), SP-005 (Elias's request — fulfilled, cost is real), SP-008 ("I will remember everything. I just won't feel it." — haunts the silence after he leaves)
- **Character knowledge inputs:** Maren knows the potion system and its cost. She has watched it work. Elias knows what he has lost — or rather, that he no longer knows what he has lost.
- **Character knowledge outputs:** Reader sees the full cost made real. Maren's choice is shown through action. The thematic question is embodied in the final image.
- **Continuity dependencies:** Depends on all previous scenes. SP-001 (hands) — now holding, not working. SP-002 (bottle) — reached for, not taken. SP-003 (smell) — behind her at the door. SP-004 (questions) — replaced by silence. SP-005 (request) — fulfilled. SP-008 ("remember everything") — haunts the silence.
