## MICRO-CRAFT RULES
- The Physical Anchor: Characters must interact with their environment. Do not state emotions directly (e.g., 'She felt anxious'). Show interaction (e.g., 'She pressed her thumbnail into the edge of the laminated folder until the plastic snapped').
- Dialogue Economy: Characters deflect, change the subject, or focus on physical tasks while talking. Ban ping-pong fact-sharing dialogue.
- Sentence Architecture: Actively vary sentence lengths. Follow long, flowing descriptive sentences with a single, sharp sentence.
- Exemplars: Obey the active book's `bible/STYLE_GUIDE.md` positive and banned exemplars. Never copy staccato repetition, noun/verb laundry lists, or mantra endings.
